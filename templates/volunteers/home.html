कार्यप्रणाली | Standard Operating Procedure

How Volunteer Operations Work

Four streamlined steps from digital registration to verified ground impact and certificate issuance.

1

Digital Registration

Enroll with basic profile, skills, contact info, and generate your official Seva ID badge.

2

Set Availability & Skills

Indicate recurring weekly slots (Mornings, Weekends) and specializations (First Aid, Logistics, Tech).

3

Join Shift & Duty Role

Select suitable campaign shifts. Get assigned specific roles like Field Marshall or Desk In-Charge.

4

Verified Certificate

Check in at site. Upon duty completion, coordinator signs off your hours and generates digital certificate.
